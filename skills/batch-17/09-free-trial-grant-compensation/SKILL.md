# Rules

- Record trial history and disallow repeat use unless a future policy explicitly approves it.
- Encrypt grant/adjustment reasons and set finite start/end times.
- Separate grant creator and approver; make every grant revocable.
- Require approval for large quota adjustments and preserve the ledger.
