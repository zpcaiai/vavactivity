---
name: vav-anti-harassment-controls
description: Detect repeated unwanted contact while protecting victims and avoiding intent claims from frequency alone.
---

Aggregate rate, distinct-target, post-decline, post-ending and block-evasion signals in versioned
windows. Frequency may rate-limit or trigger review; it never proves malicious intent. Offer immediate
block, contact revocation and support without requiring the target to contact the actor.
