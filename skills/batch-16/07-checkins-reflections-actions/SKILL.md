# Rules

- Check-ins are optional/private by default; explicit sharing is required.
- Reflections are visible only to the author and encrypted at rest.
- AI use requires an active matching consent plus relationship-context preference.
- Assigning an item to the partner creates a proposal; it is not active until the partner accepts.
- Do not compute health, compatibility, risk or success scores from answers.

# Verify

Test visibility, encryption, consent denial/revocation, action acceptance and absence of scoring fields/routes.
