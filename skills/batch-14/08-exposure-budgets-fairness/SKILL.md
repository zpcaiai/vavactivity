# Exposure budgets and fairness

Receiving and being shown are two independent daily budgets. A rendered card is not an
exposure: only a card visible long enough, or an opened profile or photo, counts. Repeat
exposure has a cooldown that expires on its own. Popular profiles are suppressed from new
exposure, never removed from existing interactions. Fairness is measured among qualified
candidates only — balancing exposure must never mean showing someone who fails the other
party's stated conditions. Membership tier may affect a member's own limits and filters; it may
never affect another person's hard constraints, safety restrictions or privacy settings.
