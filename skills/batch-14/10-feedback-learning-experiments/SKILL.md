# Feedback, tuning and experiments

Feedback events are typed and idempotent. Blocks and reports remove candidates
immediately, go to the safety domain and are never preference-learning data. A
skip starts a cooldown and never becomes a hidden hard constraint. Negative
reasons stay encrypted and are never shown to the other member. Feedback may
nudge the member's own approved feature weights inside bounded limits, and the
member can switch personalisation off or reset it while keeping their stated
criteria. Online feedback never mutates a production strategy: changes go through
offline evaluation, approval and a versioned release. Experiments pin their
strategies, assign stably, are disabled by default, need approval and stop on a
guardrail breach — click and like rates are not success metrics.
