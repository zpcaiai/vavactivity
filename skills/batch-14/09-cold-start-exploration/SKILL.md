# Cold start and exploration

A new member is served from their explicit preferences and transparent defaults. The platform
never substitutes inferred taste, appearance prediction, spend or private conversation content
for missing data. Exploration slots follow the member's own exploration setting and are drawn
from the qualified pool only: exploration varies the soft mix, never eligibility, hard
constraints, safety or the minimum bidirectional score. A newly approved profile gets a floor
of exposure among people it genuinely matches, then stops being special. Sparse-preference
guidance is advice, never a requirement — no field is ever made mandatory to receive
recommendations. An empty batch is explained honestly with aggregate reasons and real options.
