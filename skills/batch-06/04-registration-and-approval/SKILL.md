# Workflow

Whitelist form field types and reject secrets, payment data and executable
content. Lock user/activity uniqueness. Keep payment timing explicit. Free
automatic registration may confirm atomically; paid registration confirms only
after a verified admission entitlement. Never expose internal review notes.
