# Explanation generation

Explanations come from evaluated features and approved templates. Show mutual
strengths, the member's own satisfied preferences, topics to explore and
information gaps, plus the approved caveat. Never show a percentage, an internal
weight, another member's hidden preferences or their opinion of the viewer, and
never claim a probability, a guarantee or a soulmate. A relaxed criterion is
disclosed explicitly. Optional AI rewriting receives only approved items, may not
add a code or a claim, is schema-checked and falls back to the deterministic
template on any doubt.
