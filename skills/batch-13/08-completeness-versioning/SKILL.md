# Completeness and immutable versions

Completeness measures form completion only — never personal worth, marriage value, spiritual
maturity or match probability. Required fields carry a fixed share of the score so filling them
lands on the submission floor; optional detail lifts a profile toward recommendation eligibility.
A missing mandatory field blocks submission regardless of the aggregate. Snapshots preserve the
policy version. Editing during review opens a new draft while the approved version keeps serving
other members.
