# Rules

- Default lists to pseudonyms, state, timestamps and aggregate counts.
- Split normal diagnostics from invitation-content and contact-sensitive permissions.
- Require reason and purpose for high-risk read, invalidation, revoke and replay actions.
- Audit sensitive reads and every mutation.
- Do not expose a control that creates member choices, acceptance or consent.

# Verify

Cover permission denial, redaction, audited sensitive access, mutation reasons and fabrication prevention.
