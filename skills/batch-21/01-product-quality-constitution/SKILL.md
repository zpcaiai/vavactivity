---
name: vav-product-quality-constitution
description: Define or enforce VAV quality dimensions, completion rules, release principles, exception policy, ownership, and non-waivable failures.
---

Treat completion as a traceable business closure with current evidence. Preserve fail-closed rules in `quality-manifest.yaml`; never allow an aggregate score or Waiver to hide a blocker, data leak, block bypass, payment integrity failure, privacy erasure gap, unrecoverable data, or critical security finding.
