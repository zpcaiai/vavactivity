# Cold start and exploration

Classify new users, new profiles, sparse preferences, sparse regions and no
interaction history, and serve them from explicit profiles and preferences plus
transparent platform defaults. Never read behaviour of similar users, appearance,
spend, AI conversations or counselling records. Exploration slots are limited and
still satisfy every hard constraint, safety check, privacy rule and minimum
score. New approved profiles get a minimum qualified exposure chance without
being pushed past better matches. When no candidate qualifies, return an honest
empty result with the aggregate criteria that reduced the pool and the actions
the member can take — never a silent relaxation or an invented recommendation.
