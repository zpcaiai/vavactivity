# Privacy incident

## Symptoms and impact

Personal data is exposed, over-retained, misprojected, exported to the wrong user, or accessed without purpose/permission.

## Detect

Inspect sensitive-access audit, projection rules, object access, export recipients, retention/hold state, and cross-user tests using authorized investigators only.

## Immediate containment

Revoke access/links/tokens, stop affected processing, preserve audit evidence, minimize investigator access, and notify privacy leadership.

## Recovery

Correct authorization/projection, purge unauthorized copies under legal guidance, rotate keys if needed, and re-run export/erasure/consent controls.

## Verification and rollback

Prove two-user isolation, purpose logging, expiry, and cache/index/object cleanup. Keep the feature disabled if any path remains uncertain.

## Communication and review

Follow jurisdictional notification decisions; do not shame affected users or expose them in status updates. Record data categories, people, duration, action, and prevention.
