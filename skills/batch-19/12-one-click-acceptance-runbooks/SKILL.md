Use `vavctl doctor/bootstrap/up/seed/smoke/verify/backup/backup-verify/restore-drill/down`; use Make for granular gates. Validate prerequisites and scoped secrets before work, keep seed classes environment-safe, and require exact confirmation for data reset. Acceptance combines manifests, migrations, backend/frontend/security, contracts, system, complete E2E, performance and readiness. Follow the deployment/operations/runbook documents and retain `NOT_CERTIFIED` for unavailable staging, cloud, provider, backup, restore or human evidence.
