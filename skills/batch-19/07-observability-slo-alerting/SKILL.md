description: Operate privacy-minimized VAV logs, metrics, traces, dashboards, SLOs and actionable alerts.
---

Emit structured service/release/request identity and low-cardinality route templates. Recursively redact tokens, credentials, cookies, personal content and sensitive attributes before export. Trace API, database, queue and provider boundaries without payload leakage. Alert on user impact, error/latency burn, queue age, dead letters, payment confirmation, block propagation, backup age and restore evidence. Every alert links to an owned runbook; never reduce safety or privacy checks to improve latency.
