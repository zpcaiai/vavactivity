---
name: vav-quality-requirement-registry
description: Register, version, approve, supersede, import, or audit VAV requirements from plans, batch specifications, security, privacy, operations, and incidents.
---

Use immutable requirement codes, content fingerprints, explicit source versions, owner and criticality. Identical imports are no-ops; changed requirements create a governed version. An author cannot approve their own requirement, and unowned blockers fail verification.
