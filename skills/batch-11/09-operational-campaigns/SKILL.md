# Operational campaigns

Require safe audience fields, consent/suppression filtering, a test send, separated approval and an
immutable audience snapshot. Revalidate at delivery time; keep recipient idempotency, rate/batch
limits, pause/cancel semantics and honest statistics that do not imply email recall or outcomes.
