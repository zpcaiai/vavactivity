# Workflow

Separate ordinary operations, sensitive-form access, exports, check-in revoke
and one-sided-choice investigation permissions. Require reasons for rejection,
reorder, revoke, cancellation and match suspension. Default post-event views to
aggregate metrics and keep secrets out of audit presentation.
