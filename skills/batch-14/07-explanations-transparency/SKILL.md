# Explanations and transparency

Explanations are rule-and-template only, drawn from an approved sentence set. They describe
what the two members have in common, what is worth talking about and what is still unknown.
They never reveal the other person's criteria, their directional score, internal weights, a
success probability, another candidate's rank, or a past rejection. Gaps are phrased without
blame. Unexplainable features (profile readiness) never reach a member. Every card carries the
caveat that a recommendation is an opportunity to meet, not a guarantee. A separate
transparency view tells a member which data categories are used, which of their own conditions
were applied, what is never used, what they cannot see and how to adjust their own settings.
