# Hard and soft partner criteria

Only criteria approved by business, privacy and legal review may enter automated filtering.
A hard constraint must be marked `required` so its exclusion effect is explicit, and it is never
relaxed without the member's acknowledgement. Contradictory combinations are explained rather
than silently dropped. Preferences are private to the owner and the recommendation engine; no
other member may read them, and nothing is inferred from narrative text.
