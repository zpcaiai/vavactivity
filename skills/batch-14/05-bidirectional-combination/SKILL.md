# Bidirectional combination

Combine the two directional scores with a harmonic mean floored by the weaker direction and
penalised as the gap widens, so a 95/25 split can never present as a comfortable 60. The
combination is symmetric; one dead direction kills the pair. Confidence is the weaker of the
two. Mutual strengths require both sides to agree; a large asymmetry becomes a topic to
explore, not a hidden average. Both directions must clear their own floor before a pair may be
recommended — not just the combined score.
