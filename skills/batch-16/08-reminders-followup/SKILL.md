# Rules

- Require participant opt-in and a unique deduplication key.
- Respect the monthly cap and notification preferences/quiet time.
- Stop on pause, safety freeze, ending, opt-out or block.
- Wording is factual and non-manipulative; no urgency, guilt, scarcity or relationship prediction.

# Verify

Test opt-in, deduplication, cap, stop conditions and forbidden-language content cases.
